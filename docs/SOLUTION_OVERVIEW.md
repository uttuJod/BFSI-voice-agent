# Solution Overview

**One sentence.** A realtime voice agent for Indian loan collections that understands English, Hindi and Hinglish without a language selector, answers policy questions only from versioned documents, refuses to touch account data before verification, survives being interrupted, and records every commitment exactly once.

**Why it is one system.** The evaluation repository lists retrieval, interruption handling, multilingual latency and guaranteed delivery as separate problems. In a collections call they arrive in the same thirty seconds: a borrower interrupts in Hindi to say they already paid, asks what the grace period is now that it changed, and promises to pay on the fifth. Each subsystem is built and measured on its own, and the integration is where the product value is.

**How a turn works.** Browser audio streams over a WebSocket. Silero VAD marks speech; Deepgram transcribes in multi-language mode. The turn manager finalises on silence plus an ASR settle window. The language detector decides the reply language from script, a Hinglish lexicon and Deepgram's word tags. A fine-tuned Qwen3.5-4B router, served by vLLM with a LoRA adapter, returns a validated JSON decision: intent, whether RAG or a tool is needed, arguments, clarification flag. Deterministic guards then decide what is allowed: verification gate, third-party privacy block, credential refusal, date and amount validation. Policy questions go to the self-correcting RAG, which evaluates evidence, detects version conflicts, rewrites queries, and either answers with citations, clarifies or abstains. Account actions go to business tools; write tools record locally and enqueue a durable job keyed by customer, tool and arguments. The response is localised, numbers are spoken in the reply language, and Cartesia streams audio. If the caller interrupts, playback is cleared, generation is cancelled by id, and the interrupting speech opens the next turn with context intact.

**What is measured.** Router accuracy on a locked 120-case benchmark (98.33%). RAG baseline versus self-correcting on behaviour accuracy, rule-judged hallucination, retrieval recall, attribution, conflict detection, latency and iterations, on BFSI and on the evaluators' e-commerce corpus. Language detection on 54 utterances including the evaluator set (100%). Per-stage voice latency and barge-in stop latency from recorded sessions against the Problem 4 targets. Exactly-once delivery under four chaos scenarios against real worker processes.

**What it is not.** Not bank-grade authentication, not a telephony product, not multi-host, and the router is trained for BFSI only. These are stated, not hidden, and the next steps for each are in `docs/COVERAGE.md` and `docs/PRD.md`.
