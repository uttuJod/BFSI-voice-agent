# Returns Policy v2

This policy applies to all orders placed on or after 1 January 2026 and supersedes version 1.

## Return window

Most items may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Electronics, including headphones, smart watches and speakers, may be returned within 7 days of delivery provided the seal is intact and the device has not been activated.

## Conditions

- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
- Accessories, manuals and free gifts must be included.
- Innerwear, swimwear and personalised items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless the item is defective on arrival. Innerwear with the tag removed is not eligible for return.

## Footwear

Shoes may be returned within 7 days if they have only been tried on indoors and the box is undamaged.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days after the returned item passes quality check.

## How to start a return

Open the order in the app, choose "Return item" and select a reason. A pickup is scheduled within 2 working days.
