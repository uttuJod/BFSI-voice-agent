# Shipping FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in metro cities and 5 to 8 working days elsewhere. Express delivery, where available, takes 1 to 2 working days.

## Is shipping free?

Shipping is free for orders above 499 rupees. Orders below that amount are charged 49 rupees for standard delivery.

## Can I change the delivery address?

The delivery address can be changed from the app until the order is marked "shipped". After shipping, contact support and we will attempt a redirect, which is not guaranteed.

## How do I track my order?

Open the order in the app and tap "Track". The tracking page shows the carrier, the current location and the expected delivery date.

## What if my package arrives damaged?

Refuse the delivery if the outer box is visibly damaged. If you have already accepted it, raise a damage claim from the order page within 48 hours with photos.

## Do you deliver on weekends?

Carriers deliver on Saturdays in most cities. Sunday delivery is not available.
