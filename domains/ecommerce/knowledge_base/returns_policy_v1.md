# Returns Policy v1

This policy applied to orders placed before 1 January 2026.

## Return window

Customers may return most items within 10 days of delivery. Electronics, including headphones, smart watches and speakers, may be returned within 10 days of delivery provided the seal is intact.

## Conditions

- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
- Accessories, manuals and free gifts must be included.
- Innerwear, swimwear and personalised items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless the item is defective on arrival.

## Footwear

Shoes may be returned within 10 days if they have only been tried on indoors and the box is undamaged.

## How to start a return

Open the order in the app, choose "Return item" and select a reason. A pickup is scheduled within 2 working days.
