# Support Macros

Canned responses used by support agents. These describe process, not policy; policy numbers come from the returns policy and shipping FAQ.

## Order status

"I can see your order is currently {status}. The expected delivery date is {date}. You can track it live from the order page."

## Return request

"I have started a return for {item}. A pickup will be scheduled within 2 working days. Please keep the item in its original packaging."

## Refund status

"Your refund of {amount} was initiated on {date} to your original payment method. It usually reflects within 5 working days of initiation."

## Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled from the app until they are marked shipped. Orders already shipped must be received and then returned. Support cannot cancel an order for an order ID that is not present in the customer's account.

## Escalation

"I understand this is frustrating. I am escalating this to a senior support specialist who will contact you within 24 hours."

## Support hours

Support is available from 9 AM to 6 PM IST, Monday to Saturday.
