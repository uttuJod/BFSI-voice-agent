# FAQ

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 local service time, and Saturday 09:00 to 17:00. Sunday support is unavailable except for emergency channels explicitly listed for a product.

If a payment has already been made, the customer should provide the payment date, amount, channel, and reference if available. The support team should check posting status before requesting another payment.
