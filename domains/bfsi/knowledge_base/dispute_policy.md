# Payment Disputes

If a customer says a payment cleared but internal records do not show it, support should verify identity and collect transaction evidence such as date, amount, payment channel, and available reference number.

The case may then be opened as a payment dispute for investigation. Agents must not claim that the payment is missing until the investigation verifies the discrepancy.
