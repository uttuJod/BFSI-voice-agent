# Human Escalation

Escalate to a human specialist when the customer requests a human, when policy evidence is conflicting or insufficient for a safe decision, or when the issue requires an account-specific action unavailable to the automated support layer.

Callbacks may be offered during support hours where operationally available. Do not promise an exact callback time unless the scheduling system confirms one.
