# Approved Support Language

When evidence is insufficient: "I don't have enough verified policy information to answer that safely."

When clarification is required: "Could you clarify which account issue or payment situation you mean?"

When conflicting active policy cannot be resolved: "I found conflicting policy information and can't safely choose between the sources. This needs review."
