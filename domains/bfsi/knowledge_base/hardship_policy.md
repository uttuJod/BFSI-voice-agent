# Financial Hardship

Customers who cannot meet a scheduled repayment because of unemployment, reduced income, illness, emergency expenses, or another temporary financial shock may request hardship review.

Support should first verify identity, understand the customer's circumstances, and assess available repayment options. Possible outcomes include a temporary reduced-payment plan, changed payment date, short deferral, or referral to a specialist hardship team.

No specific hardship outcome is guaranteed before review. Agents must not promise approval.
