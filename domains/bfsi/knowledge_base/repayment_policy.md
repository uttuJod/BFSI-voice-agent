# Repayment Options

Customers may make a full payment or an eligible partial payment. A partial payment does not automatically change the contractual due date or remove overdue status.

Where eligible, a structured repayment arrangement may split an overdue amount across agreed instalments. Eligibility depends on account status and affordability review.
